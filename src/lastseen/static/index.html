Welcome

lastseen keeps a private history of where your Apple devices have been, so you can see where a lost device was last seen. Everything stays on this computer. Not affiliated with Apple.

  • Works with the devices on your Apple Account and your Family Sharing group.
  • Checks in the background every few minutes.
  • Setup takes about two minutes.
